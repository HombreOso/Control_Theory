# Control Systems Brief — General Control Systems — 2026-09-29

## Research Papers
- "Fundamental Limits of Adaptive Stabilization with an Unknown Growth Exponent" derives theoretical bounds on how well adaptive controllers can stabilize a nonlinear system when its growth rate is not known in advance. [arXiv:2609.02740](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02740)
- "Self-Adaptive Learning and Model Predictive Control for Tracking Unknown Dynamics with No Regret" combines online learning with MPC to track systems with unknown dynamics while providing no-regret performance guarantees. [arXiv:2607.26370](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26370)
