# Control Systems Brief — General Control Systems — 2026-09-14

## Tech News
- SMC expanded its EX600 field I/O platform with a new EXW1-RD wireless I/O remote and integrated PROFIsafe support, letting automation engineers wirelessly connect robot tooling (e.g., grippers) while carrying safety-critical signals over industrial Ethernet. [SMC Expands EX600 With Wireless I/O](https://control.com/news/smc-expands-ex600-with-wireless-i-o-and-new-profisafe-support/)
- KEB America introduced "One Cable Technology" (OCT), combining motor power and encoder feedback into a single cable for its S6 servo drives and F6 VFDs, simplifying wiring and control-cabinet design for multi-axis machines. [KEB One Cable Technology](https://control.com/news/keb-one-cable-technology-combines-motor-power-and-feedback/)

## Research Papers
- "An Adaptive Multi-Parameter ADMM Algorithm for Embedded MPC," accepted for CDC 2026, proposes an adaptive ADMM scheme for solving embedded model predictive control problems more efficiently on constrained hardware. [arXiv:2609.12992](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12992)
- "Safe Stabilising Full-Order Affine Control Barrier Functions for Linear Systems (Extended)" develops control barrier function constructions that guarantee both safety and stabilization for linear systems. [arXiv:2609.12990](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12990)
- "Very Exciting: Zero-Shot Model Predictive Control of Buildings via Excitation-Based Generalized Transfer Learning Models" combines MPC with transfer-learning building models to enable zero-shot deployment of building energy controllers. [arXiv:2609.12853](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12853)
- "Adaptive Agent Design" examines adaptive control-theoretic agent design at the intersection of control, game theory, and optimization. [arXiv:2609.12486](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12486)
- "Exact PID and PI Gain Regions for Uncertain Non-Affine MIMO Systems" (submitted to IEEE Transactions on Automatic Control) derives exact stabilizing gain regions for PID/PI controllers applied to uncertain non-affine multi-input multi-output systems. [arXiv:2609.11130](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11130)
- "Construction of Control Lyapunov-Barrier Functions from CLF-CBF Pairs" presents a method for building combined Lyapunov-barrier functions from existing control Lyapunov function/control barrier function pairs, relevant to safe adaptive/robust control design. [arXiv:2609.11746](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11746)
