# Control Systems Brief — General Control Systems — 2026-09-30

## Software & Tools
- MathWorks released **MATLAB and Simulink R2026b**, adding a Simulink Variant Manager, a Visual Inspection Toolbox, and Polyspace Test Server, plus performance upgrades to Aerospace Blockset (guidance, navigation and control, mission planning, system-level performance analysis) and distributed multi-core acceleration for Simulink Design Verifier — both relevant to control-system verification and validation workflows. [MathWorks Introduces Release 2026b of MATLAB and Simulink](https://www.mathworks.com/company/newsroom/mathworks-introduces-release-2026b-of-matlab-and-simulink.html) (Sept 24, 2026)

## Research Papers
- "Robust Variable-Horizon MPC for Landing a Multirotor UAV on a Moving Platform" proposes a variable-horizon robust MPC scheme for autonomous UAV landing on moving platforms. [arXiv:2609.34574](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34574)
- "Reducing Combinatorial Redundancy in Mixed-Integer MPC via Ranking-Based Feasible-Set Restriction" and its companion paper "RFSR-MI-MPC: Ranking-Based Feasible-Set Restriction for Real-Time Control of Reconfigurable Battery Packs" introduce a ranking-based method to cut the combinatorial search space in mixed-integer MPC for real-time use. [arXiv:2609.32029](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.32029) / [arXiv:2609.32032](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.32032)
- "Statistical Learning of Contractive Dynamical Representations for Composite Adaptive Control" uses statistically learned contractive representations to design composite adaptive controllers with stability guarantees. [arXiv:2609.35758](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35758)
- "Neural ODEs Meet Concurrent Learning: Stable Online Learning with Lyapunov Guarantees" combines neural ODEs with concurrent learning to give Lyapunov-backed stability guarantees for online adaptive control. [arXiv:2609.32289](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.32289)
- "Two-Timescale Reinforcement Learning for Real-Time Optimization and Economic NMPC" proposes a two-timescale RL scheme to bridge real-time optimization with economic nonlinear MPC. [arXiv:2609.35141](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35141)
