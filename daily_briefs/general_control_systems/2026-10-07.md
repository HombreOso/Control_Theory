## Tech News
- Control Engineering's "PID spotlight" series explains PID gap control — tuning a controller to respond weakly near setpoint but strongly as error grows, via gain-shaping or error-signal modification. [Control Engineering](https://www.controleng.com/pid-spotlight-part-31-what-is-pid-gap-control/)

## Research Papers
- "On Global Regulatability of Robot Manipulators by Classical PID" proves that while PID with the right gains guarantees global stability for single-DOF manipulators, the authors construct a multi-DOF manipulator for which no PID gain choice achieves global regulation — resolving a long-standing open question. [arXiv:2609.01207](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01207)
