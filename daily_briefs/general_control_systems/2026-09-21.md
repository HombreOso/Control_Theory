# Control Systems Brief — General Control Systems — 2026-09-21
_Note: results reflect the latest available coverage rather than being precisely date-scoped._

## Use Cases
- A nonlinear, model-free Data-Enabled Predictive Control (DeePC) scheme was deployed and simulated for real residential home heating, demonstrating that data-driven predictive control (no explicit physical model) can regulate home comfort in practice. [Model-Free Control for Residential Heating: Deployment and Simulation of Nonlinear Data-Enabled Predictive Control (arXiv:2609.21500)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21500)

## Research Papers
- A new stochastic MPC formulation targets systems facing heavy-tailed (non-Gaussian) disturbances, using extreme value theory to derive less conservative constraint tightening than classical Gaussian-based robust MPC. [Stochastic MPC under Heavy-Tailed Disturbances: An Extreme Value Theory Approach (arXiv:2609.21170)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21170)
- Researchers propose an event-triggered linear-quadratic-Gaussian (LQG) control scheme for multi-agent systems sharing a bandwidth-limited communication channel, cutting communication load while preserving performance guarantees. [Multi-Agent Event-Triggered LQG Control under Shared Communication Constraints (arXiv:2609.21974)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21974)
- An adaptive, uncertainty-aware radial-basis-function predictive control scheme is applied to personalized fluid resuscitation, extending MPC-style techniques to patient-specific physiological regulation in critical care. [Adaptive Uncertainty-Aware Modeling and Stochastic Radial Basis Function Predictive Control for Personalized Fluid Resuscitation (arXiv:2609.21821)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21821)
- A "runtime-incremental transformer" architecture is proposed for reinforcement-learning-based adaptive control, letting controllers update online as new data arrives during deployment rather than only at training time. [Runtime-Incremental Transformer for Reinforcement-Learning-Based Adaptive Control (arXiv:2609.13545)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13545)
- New work derives optimal regret bounds for online control of storage systems (e.g. batteries) via cumulative policies, connecting online-learning regret theory with optimal control of storage/inventory dynamics. [Optimal Regret for Online Storage Control via Cumulative Policies (arXiv:2609.21262)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21262)
