[Robotics Tomorrow, Aug 31, 2026](https://www.roboticstomorrow.com/news/2026/08/31/higen-rnm-targets-humanoid-robotics-next-bottleneck-the-actuator-/27016/)

## Use Cases
- **Grid battery frequency-response control as attack surface.** Real-world illustration of how legitimate PID/governor-style frequency-response logic in grid-scale batteries can be weaponized, underscoring the practical stakes of controller tuning and monitoring in critical infrastructure. - **Humanoid joint-level distributed control.** HIGEN RNM's actuator platform is a concrete applied case of moving from centralized to distributed/local closed-loop control at each robot joint to meet real-time response and safety requirements. ## Research Papers
- **"Accelerating Reinforcement Learning via MPC Solver-Gradient Guidance for Weights-varying MPC (SG-RL)"** — combines RL with bounded solver-derived gradients from an MPC solver to adapt cost-function weights online; tested on autonomous racing, matching baseline RL with far fewer samples. [arXiv:2609.01061](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01061)
- **"On the Degree of Safety: Beyond Safe or Unsafe with Control Barrier Functions"** — argues standard CBFs give only binary safe/unsafe classifications and proposes "invariance authority demand" as a representation-independent safety-margin measure. Authors include Magnus Egerstedt. [arXiv:2609.03319](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03319)
- **"Robust stabilization with spiking neuronal communication"** — framework for robust stabilization when sensor-to-controller communication uses neuromorphic spike encoding/decoding, with practical ISS design conditions validated on a single-link manipulator. [arXiv:2609.05054](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05054)
- **"Headroom-Aware Stochastic Adaptive Model Predictive Control for Load Frequency Control in Microgrids"** — adaptive MPC for microgrid frequency regulation accounting for available control "headroom" under uncertainty. [arXiv:2609.05332](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05332)
- **"Risk-Aware Optimal Control with Rulebooks"** — optimal control formulation incorporating structured risk constraints ("rulebooks") into the control objective. [arXiv:2609.05199](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05199)
- **"Open-Loop Stackelberg LQ Difference Games with Coupled-Affine Inequality Constraints"** — extends linear-quadratic differential/difference game theory to constrained Stackelberg (leader-follower) settings. [arXiv:2609.04928](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04928)
