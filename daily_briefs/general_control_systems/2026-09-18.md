## Research Papers
- New theoretical work characterizes bifurcation behavior near surface-tangential points in continuous sliding-mode control, going beyond standard asymptotic-convergence assumptions to better describe real closed-loop behavior near the sliding surface. [Bifurcation Beyond Surface-Tangential Asymptotic Convergence in Continuous Sliding Mode Control (arXiv:2609.19682)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19682)
- A paper on discrete-time projected control systems establishes incremental stability and convergence properties, providing theoretical guarantees useful for constrained controller design. [Incremental Stability and Convergence Properties of Discrete-Time Projected Control Systems (arXiv:2609.20955)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20955)
- Researchers derive rate-cost trade-offs for H-infinity control when the initial system state is uncertain, characterizing fundamental limits between communication/information rate and achievable control performance. [Rate-cost Trade-offs in H-infinity Control with Initial State Uncertainty (arXiv:2609.21270)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21270)
