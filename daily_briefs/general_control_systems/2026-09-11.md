# Control Systems Brief — General Control Systems — 2026-09-11

## Use Cases
- A hybrid MPC-PID controller for UAV dynamic systems is reported to improve robustness/performance over either method alone under disturbance and uncertainty, validated on UAV flight dynamics. [Scientific Reports](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-32436-6) · [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12820076/)
- A robust model-reference adaptive controller is demonstrated on a 3-DOF planar manipulator, targeting trajectory tracking under model uncertainty. [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12873312/)

## Research Papers
- "Real-Time Model Predictive Control Algorithms for Autonomous Spacecraft Guidance" (submitted Sept 1, 2026) benchmarks four MPC algorithm families for spacecraft rendezvous, shows gradient-based solvers can fail at long horizons due to ill-conditioning, and validates Tube MPC with from-scratch Cholesky/Riccati solvers as flight-software-viable. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00927)
- "Model predictive control: past, present, and future" — a 2026 review in *Control Theory and Technology* tracing MPC's development and current research frontiers. [Springer](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11768-026-00320-x)
- "A novel nonlinear PID controller design with adaptive gains" (2026) proposes an adaptive-gain nonlinear PID scheme aimed at improving performance over fixed-gain PID across operating conditions. [Scientific Reports](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-026-47124-2)
- "Reinforcement Learning for Control Systems with Time Delays: A Comprehensive Survey" reviews RL methods applied to delayed/time-lagged control-system dynamics. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.00399v1)
- "Robust adaptive MPC using control contraction metrics" combines contraction-based robustness certificates with adaptive MPC for nonlinear systems with parametric uncertainty. [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0005109823003308)
