## Research Papers
- Fast-DeePC replaces hard equality constraints in data-enabled predictive control with a soft ridge penalty, giving a smaller online problem solved faster by a custom interior-point solver. [arXiv:2610.12902](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12902)
- A scalable learned iterative solver for nonlinear MPC uses a horizon-independent model predicting KKT-directed steps, reporting up to 4x speedup over IPOPT on a CSTR cascade. [arXiv:2610.13480](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13480)
- Distributionally robust adaptive iterative covariance steering combines covariance steering with L1 adaptive control, cutting keep-out violations from 32.7% to 0.2% in Monte Carlo trials (spacecraft proximity operations). [arXiv:2610.12774](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12774)
- Direct Vector Field Guidance for Path Following proposes a tanh-based course-angle guidance law for turn-rate-limited UAVs with Lyapunov convergence analysis. [arXiv:2610.13576](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13576)
- Anti-windup techniques for amplitude and rate saturation (revised version) modify PID and LQI controllers and benchmark them against constrained MPC on AUV yaw control. [arXiv:2601.01302](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.01302)
