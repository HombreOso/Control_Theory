## Research Papers
- "World Motion Models: Flexible Sequence Modeling of SE(3) Trajectories" (UC Berkeley) proposes a unified SE(3)-trajectory sequence model via flow-matching, reducing tasks like future prediction, inverse kinematics, and model-predictive control to masked inference over one shared representation. [arXiv:2610.01742](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01742)
- "Predefined-Time Integral Reinforcement Learning for Unknown Nonlinear Systems via Inverse-Optimal Design" combines adaptive/RL-based control with predefined-time convergence guarantees for unknown nonlinear plants. [arXiv:2610.12103](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12103)
