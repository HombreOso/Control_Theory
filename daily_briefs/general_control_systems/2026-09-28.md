# Control Systems Brief — General Control Systems — 2026-09-28

## Research Papers
- "Koopman-Based Robust Model Predictive Control for Nonlinear Systems with Stochastic Intermittent Measurements" (Liu, Wu, Zhang, Du, Han) develops a data-driven Koopman-operator MPC scheme that stays robust when sensor measurements arrive intermittently at random. [arXiv:2609.02079](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02079)
- "Real-Time Model Predictive Control Algorithms for Autonomous Spacecraft Guidance" (Garab) presents real-time-capable MPC formulations aimed at onboard spacecraft guidance. [arXiv:2609.00927](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00927)
- "Fundamental Limits of Adaptive Stabilization with an Unknown Growth Exponent" (Liu) establishes theoretical limits on how fast adaptive controllers can stabilize systems when the plant's growth rate is unknown. [arXiv:2609.02740](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02740)
- "Generalized Semi-Infinite Programming for Robust Optimal Control with Decision-Dependent Uncertainty" (Wehbeh, Kerrigan, Scaccia) extends robust optimal control to cases where the uncertainty set itself depends on the control decisions. [arXiv:2609.01538](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01538)
- "A simple derivation of the Kalman filter" (Chiani, Petris, Win), forthcoming in *IEEE Signal Processing Magazine*, offers a streamlined derivation intended as a cleaner teaching reference than the standard treatments. [arXiv:2609.02332](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02332)
- "Model predictive control: past, present, and future" (*Control Theory and Technology*) is a broad retrospective-and-outlook review tracing MPC's development and where the field is headed next. [Springer](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11768-026-00320-x)
