## Tech News
- Schneider Electric's **EcoCare for BMS** is an AI-driven continuous monitoring service that layers digital tracking, analytics, and remote/on-site support onto existing building management systems to flag operational issues before they disrupt performance. Schneider cites up to 25% lower facility energy consumption, 30% better operational efficiency, and 45% less downtime; the service is being pitched hardest at healthcare and higher-education facilities. [Facilities Dive](https://www.facilitiesdive.com/news/schneider-electric-launches-ai-driven-continuous-bms-monitoring-service/822148/)

## Research Papers
- Saloux et al., "From theory to practice: A critical review of model predictive control field implementations in the built environment" (*Applied Energy*), reviews 91 real-world MPC deployments in buildings over 19 years. Reported average savings: ~30% thermal energy, ~25% electricity, ~25% energy cost, with most trials in buildings under 10,000 m² and averaging 41 days. The review flags standardized performance evaluation as the main barrier still holding back wider MPC adoption in BMS. [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0306261925008219)
