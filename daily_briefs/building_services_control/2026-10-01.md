# Control Systems Brief — Building Services Control — 2026-10-01

## Tech News

- A detailed industry piece argues BMS analytics failures are usually baked in years earlier at the tender-specification stage: closed-by-default gateways (specs never require open BACnet access), inconsistent point naming across subcontractors (up to eleven different naming patterns for the same point type), and networks sized with no headroom for future sensors/submeters. Proposed fixes include Project Haystack/Brick Schema semantic tagging, explicit time-limited write-access controls with audit logging, and vendor-neutral specs tested for bias. [BMS Specification Requirements: Where Analytics Failures Start](https://automatedbuildings.com/2026/09/bms-specification-requirements) (Sept 25, 2026)

## New Inventions

- Priva introduced a "Cybersecurity Drop-In" consultation service at the Smart Buildings Show (ExCeL London, Oct 14–15, 2026), offering building owners/operators and BMS professionals guided assessments of compliance with NIS2, IEC 62443, and the Cyber Resilience Act, and whether existing BMS hardware meets current security requirements. [Priva launches Cybersecurity Drop-In at Smart Buildings Show 2026](https://fmuk-online.co.uk/priva-launches-cybersecurity-drop-in-at-smart-buildings-show-2026) (Sept 29, 2026)

## Use Cases

- A Schneider Electric study modeled a 53,800 sq ft three-story office prototype across three climates (Brisbane, Miami, Mumbai) and found AI-enabled BMS cut HVAC energy use by 15–27% and whole-building energy by up to 22%, with annual utility savings of $13,600–$49,300 per building and up to 60 metric tons of avoided CO2 per facility per year — roughly double the savings of smart controls alone. [Schneider Electric finds AI-enabled buildings can cut energy use by up to 22%](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/21/3365434/0/en/schneider-electric-finds-ai-enabled-buildings-can-cut-energy-use-by-up-to-22-save-on-annual-utility-costs-and-carbon.html) (Sept 21, 2026); coverage: [Study: AI-Enabled BMS Cuts HVAC Energy Use by Up to 27%](https://www.achrnews.com/articles/166716-study-ai-enabled-bms-cuts-hvac-energy-use-by-up-to-27) (Sept 23, 2026)
