# Control Systems Brief — Building Services Control — 2026-09-14

## Tech News
- ASHRAE-commissioned research from the University of New Hampshire finds BACnet-enabled building automation systems have prevented 1.4 billion tons of CO2 emissions since 1995 (about $266B in avoided climate costs), with another 2.06 billion tons preventable by 2030 through wider adoption. [$266B in building carbon costs saved thanks to BACnet, study shows](https://www.facilitiesdive.com/news/266b-in-building-carbon-costs-saved-thanks-to-bacnet-study-shows/817331/)
- A September 10, 2026 industry piece argues BMS platforms aren't being replaced by AI but are gaining an "open edge layer" for analytics and AI, letting legacy controls and new intelligence coexist rather than forcing rip-and-replace upgrades. [The BMS Isn't Going Away. It's Becoming Part of Something Bigger.](https://www.automatedbuildings.com/2026/09/the-bms-isnt-going-away-its-becoming-part-of-something-bigger/)
- A September 1, 2026 article details why BACnet SC (Secure Connect) is becoming the security standard for building automation, driven by IT/OT convergence, federal (DoD) specifications mandating it, and growing manufacturer pursuit of IEC 62443-4-2 Security Level 2 certification. [Why BACnet SC Is Becoming the Standard for Secure Building Automation](https://albireoenergy.com/2026/09/01/why-bacnet-sc-is-becoming-the-standard-for-secure-building-automation/)
