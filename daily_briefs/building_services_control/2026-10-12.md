# Control Systems Brief — Building Services Control — 2026-10-12

*Note: exact-date coverage for this topic was thin this week; some items below are the most current available material from recent weeks rather than strictly dated to this day.*

## Tech News
- Nozomi Networks analyzes the August 2026 Winnipeg Health Sciences Centre ransomware attack that disrupted HVAC and door-access controls, arguing building management systems are "clinical systems" too — often unpatched, reliant on legacy BACnet, with unclear ownership. [Nozomi Networks](https://www.nozominetworks.com/blog/when-ransomware-turns-off-the-hvac-lessons-from-the-winnipeg-hospital-incident)

## New Inventions
- Eaton unveiled Brightlayer Energy, AI-driven building energy management software that forecasts demand, coordinates solar/battery storage and EV charging, and tracks emissions; pilots cited include an Amsterdam hotel cutting electricity costs over 25%. [AccessWire](https://www.accessnewswire.com/newsroom/en/industrial-and-manufacturing/eaton-unveils-brightlayer-energy-an-ai-powered-energy-management-and-o-1149242)

## Use Cases
- Winnipeg's Health Sciences Centre suffered a ransomware attack affecting facility maintenance systems including HVAC and door-access controls; clinical care continued but the incident became a widely cited case study for BAS/IT segmentation and ownership gaps. [GovInfoSecurity](https://www.govinfosecurity.com/ransomware-attack-disables-canadian-hospitals-doors-hvac-a-32535)

## Research Papers
- "ThermoLLM: Thermodynamics-Aware HVAC Control with Spatial-Semantic Knowledge Graph" proposes an LLM-based HVAC control approach grounded in thermodynamic reasoning and a spatial-semantic knowledge graph of the building. [arXiv:2606.22911](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.22911)
