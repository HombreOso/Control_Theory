## Research Papers
- Three nonlinear data-driven predictive control methods (Select-DPC, gain-scheduling DPC, and LPV-DPC) are proposed for residential heating that avoid manually modeling each building; field validation on a Swiss research building showed roughly 11% seasonal energy savings versus standard hysteresis control. [Model-Free Control for Residential Heating: Deployment and Simulation of Nonlinear Data-Enabled Predictive Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21500)
