# Control Systems Brief — Building Services Control — 2026-09-25

## Tech News
- [The BMS Isn't Going Away. It's Becoming Part of Something Bigger.](https://www.automatedbuildings.com/2026/09/the-bms-isnt-going-away-its-becoming-part-of-something-bigger/) (Sept 10, 2026) — Argues most building owners can't afford wholesale BMS replacement, so the industry is shifting toward an "open edge layer" that bolts AI/analytics and predictive maintenance onto existing BMS infrastructure, with AI handling tedious data-tagging/normalization while engineers handle complex integration.

Building automation grew 8% in Q1 2026 vs. 2% company-wide, driven by data-center demand for liquid cooling and behind-the-meter power/controls.

## Use Cases
- [$266B in building carbon costs saved thanks to BACnet, study shows](https://www.facilitiesdive.com/news/266b-in-building-carbon-costs-saved-thanks-to-bacnet-study-shows/817331/) (Facilities Dive, on a University of New Hampshire study commissioned by ASHRAE) — Found BACnet-enabled BMS interoperability has prevented 1.4 billion tons of CO2 since 1995 (roughly $266B in avoided climate costs), with about 70% of U.S. savings from reduced electricity use; projects another 2.06 billion tons preventable by 2030 with wider adoption.

## Research Papers
- [Generalizing HVAC Control With Domain Randomized Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05822) (arXiv, submitted Sept 5, 2026) — Introduces NOMAD-RL, an RL controller using physics-informed normalizing flows for domain randomization so a single policy transfers across different building thermal zones without per-site retraining. Claims a 67.9% cost reduction vs. fixed-setpoint PID and within 14.5% of optimal MPC in multi-zone tests; note reviewers flagged weak evaluation rigor (single runs, no error bars, possible test-set leakage).
- [Safe Deep Reinforcement Learning for Energy-Efficient HVAC Control in Multi-Zone Residential Buildings](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17235) (arXiv, Aug 2026) — Applies safety-constrained deep RL to multi-zone residential HVAC control, aiming to cut energy use while keeping thermal-comfort/safety bounds enforced — relevant to evaluating RL vs. classical PID/MPC for retrofit applications.
- [Hierarchical Control Framework Integrating LLMs with RL for Decarbonized HVAC Operation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.26050) (arXiv, March 2026) — Proposes a two-layer architecture pairing an LLM-based supervisory layer with lower-level RL control for HVAC decarbonization, illustrating the emerging trend of combining LLM reasoning with classical RL/control-loop execution in building systems.
