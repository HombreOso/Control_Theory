# Control Systems Brief — Building Services Control — 2026-09-30

## Tech News
- "Digital Building Landscape Converging with AI" argues the industry is moving past theoretical AI talk into practical deployment, centered on edge intelligence moving into local controllers, semantic data standardization (Project Haystack, Brick, ASHRAE 223P) as a prerequisite for autonomous operation, buildings acting as active grid nodes, and zero-trust/secure BACnet routing as IT/OT converge. [Digital Building Landscape Converging with AI](https://www.automatedbuildings.com/2026/09/digital-building-landscape-converging-with-ai/) (Sept 3, 2026)
- AutomatedBuildings.com's editorial board formally distinguished "BAS" (the system) from "building automation" (the broader industry), reflecting how the field has expanded to include cloud platforms, cybersecurity, energy systems, AI, and data infrastructure beyond classic controls work. [BAS isn't going away… the industry just got bigger](https://www.automatedbuildings.com/2026/09/bas-isnt-going-away-the-industry-just-got-bigger/) (Sept 2, 2026)

## Software & Tools
- Emerson unveiled the **Ovation Curation Tool**, a configuration-management platform that automates synchronization between physical plant control networks and simulated/digital-twin testing environments, tracking configuration drift and running change-impact analysis before changes reach production (aimed at power generation and water management, but directly applicable to large building-services control networks facing similar drift problems). [Control System Synchronization Software for Digital Twin Architectures](https://www.usa-automation.net/news/115318-control-system-synchronization-software-for-digital-twin-architectures) (Sept 28, 2026)

## Research Papers
- "Towards Machine Learning-based Model Predictive Control for HVAC Control in Multi-Context Buildings at Scale via Ensemble Learning" develops an ensemble-learning MPC approach intended to generalize HVAC control across many building contexts without per-building retraining. [arXiv:2505.02439](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.02439v2)
- "ThermoForce: A Physics-Structured Interventional World Model for Building HVAC Control" combines physics-structured modeling with a learned world model to improve intervention planning for HVAC control. [arXiv:2607.03942](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.03942v1)
- "Small HVAC Control Demonstrations in Larger Buildings Often Overestimate Savings" is a cautionary empirical study showing that HVAC control savings measured in small pilot demonstrations frequently fail to scale proportionally to larger buildings. [arXiv:2602.22499](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.22499v1)
