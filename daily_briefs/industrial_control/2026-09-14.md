## New Inventions
- YC-backed startup Neuron Industries launched Cortex AIC, a real-time industrial controller programmed in Python and plain-English prompts via AI agents, aimed at replacing PLCs that have gone largely unchanged since the 1990s; it bundles an IDE, operator interface, digital-twin simulation, and air-gapped operation, with paid pilots underway and general availability targeted for Q4 2026. [Neuron Industries Launch — GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/08/24/3350040/0/en/yc-backed-neuron-industries-launches-to-build-a-new-industrial-controller-as-the-engineers-who-program-the-old-ones-retire.html)
- Mitsubishi Electric introduced the MX Controller, targeting simplified high-speed machine control for smarter manufacturing lines. [Mitsubishi Electric Introduces MX Controller](https://us.mitsubishielectric.com/fa/en/news-and-events/2026/july/mx-controller-pr/)

## Research Papers
- "Distributed Optimization of Modular Production Systems using Model-based Reinforcement Learning with Inverse Models" combines model-based RL with approximate inverse process models to control flexible manufacturing systems, decoupling actuation dynamics from state-space dynamics for faster, more robust training on a modular production testbed. [arXiv:2609.11615](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11615)
- "From Learning to Control: Data-Driven Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning for Multivariable Control in a Microalgae Bioprocess" proposes a hybrid ML framework where autonomous agents, trained offline on historical data, jointly regulate pH and dissolved oxygen in bioreactors and adapt online without a first-principles process model. [arXiv:2609.09313](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09313)
