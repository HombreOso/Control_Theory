## Research Papers
- "PLCBench: Can Autonomous LLM Agents Turn PLC Access into Sustained Physical Impact?" connects real commercial PLCs to closed-loop process simulations to test whether LLM agents can convert network access into lasting physical-process effects; across 5 LLM families and 240 episodes, 31.3% of agent runs sustained their physical objective. [arXiv:2608.26882](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.26882)
