# Control Systems Brief — Industrial Control — 2026-10-12

*Note: exact-date coverage for this topic was thin this week; some items below are the most current available material from recent weeks rather than strictly dated to this day.*

## New Inventions
- ABB released its "e-Device" bring-your-own-device robot interface, letting technicians use personal devices to interact with robot controllers. [Control.com](https://control.com/news/bring-your-own-device-byod-abb-releases-e-device-robot-interface/)
- Banner Engineering introduced the RSr, a machine-mountable safety relay. [Control.com](https://control.com/news/banner-engineering-introduces-machine-mountable-rsr-safety-relay/)

## Software & Tools
- Inductive Automation detailed its "Ignition 2027" roadmap, including a new feature called "Catalyst" aimed at cutting development effort, a Beckhoff ADS driver, an AWS connector module, and improved partial import/export for DevOps workflows. [DMC Inc.](https://www.dmcinfo.com/?p=52569)
- A Control Engineering op-ed argues for standardizing remote I/O as a reusable platform decoupled from PLC brand, swapping only the communication coupler when changing controllers. [Control Engineering](https://www.controleng.com/how-to-design-once-deploy-everywhere-move-past-controller-centric-i-o/)
