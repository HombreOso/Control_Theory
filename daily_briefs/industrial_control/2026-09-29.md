## Research Papers
- "Autonomous Model Lifecycle Management for Digital Twin-Based Manufacturing Control" describes a closed-loop cyber-physical system, deployed at automotive plants since 2023, that pairs a physics-based digital twin with a reinforcement-learning control policy and retrains/evaluates competing model variants automatically. Embedding operator-trust constraints mattered in practice — without them 23% of technically-accurate policies were rejected by human operators — and the system reported 28–45% process-stability gains with zero safety incidents. [arXiv:2609.22701](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22701) (submitted Sept 19, 2026)
- "Information-Theoretic Digital Twins for Stealthy Attack Detection in Industrial Control Systems" proposes a closed-form KL-divergence approach for detecting stealthy cyberattacks on ICS using digital-twin models. [arXiv:2603.01621](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.01621)
