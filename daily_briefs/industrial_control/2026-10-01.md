# Control Systems Brief — Industrial Control — 2026-10-01

## Tech News

- ABI Research named six vendors as leaders in its first Software-Defined Automation (SDA) ranking, assessing 13 industrial automation vendors on innovation and implementation: Schneider Electric (#1), Siemens (#2), Bosch Rexroth (#3), plus SUPCON, Honeywell Technologies, and CODESYS. SDA — software-based, hardware-agnostic control platforms — is projected to grow from $500M in 2025 to $4.7B by 2035 (22.3% CAGR), with automotive, food & beverage, semiconductors, and data centers as high-potential segments. [Schneider Electric, Siemens, Bosch Rexroth, SUPCON, Honeywell, CODESYS named SDA leaders](https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/29/3370961/0/en/schneider-electric-siemens-bosch-rexroth-supcon-honeywell-technologies-and-codesys-named-leaders-in-abi-research-s-software-defined-automation-sda-ranking.html) (Sept 29, 2026)
- September's ICS Patch Tuesday addressed critical flaws across major vendors: Siemens SIMATIC S7-1200/S7-1500 (privileged code execution), Schneider Electric Modicon PLCs and EcoStruxure (protocol input-validation issues enabling DoS or unauthorized config changes), AVEVA HMI/SCADA platforms (privilege escalation), and Rockwell Automation ControlLogix/CompactLogix (Ethernet/IP message handling enabling remote code execution). [ICS Patch Tuesday September 2026](https://www.maxwellplc.com/blogs/news/ics-patch-tuesday-september-2026-schneider-siemens-aveva-rockwell-patch-critical-ot-vulnerabilities) (Sept 18, 2026)
- A new market report projects the global industrial control systems market to reach $448.33 billion by 2035 at an 8.07% CAGR, citing grid modernization and industrial digitalization as key drivers. [Industrial Control Systems Market Size to Hit $448.33 Billion by 2035](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/30/3371713/0/en/industrial-control-systems-market-size-to-hit-448-33-billion-by-2035-at-8-07-cagr-research-by-sns-insider.html) (Sept 30, 2026)
