## Use Cases
- **University of Michigan + Arizona State University launch a Center for Digital Twins in Manufacturing**, with GM, Honeywell, Intel, Applied Materials, Applied Dynamics, and the U.S. Army Ground Vehicle Systems Center as industry partners. Four focus areas: physical/simulation model synchronization for fault ID, metal-additive-manufacturing defect detection, foundation models for historical process data, and "digital worker twins." [University of Michigan, Sept 4, 2026](https://news.engin.umich.edu/2026/09/academic-and-industry-center-to-solve-digital-twin-challenges-for-manufacturing/)
- **Digital-twin edge-cloud fault diagnosis deployed on hydropower turbines** (China Energy Science and Technology Research Institute). A physics-geometry-behavior digital twin achieved 56ms edge-cloud response and 98.2% fault-diagnosis accuracy on dual Pelton units, cutting maintenance costs 17.3%, downtime losses 38.1%, and risk expense 32.6% vs. periodic maintenance. [Bioengineer, Sept 4, 2026](https://bioengineer.org/digital-twins-enable-edge-cloud-fault-diagnosis-for-hydropower-equipment/)

- **"Explainable Hybrid Intrusion Detection for SCADA/ICS: A Review and Research Agenda"** (Skrodelis & Romanovs, Riga Technical University) — synthesizes 40 studies (2014–2025); notes hybrid IDS hit only 73% accuracy on zero-day attacks despite 90–99% on known threats, and only 2/10 recent systems use protocol-aware detection or SHAP explainability. [Frontiers in Computer Science, Mar 3, 2026](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/computer-science/articles/10.3389/fcomp.2026.1724245/full)
