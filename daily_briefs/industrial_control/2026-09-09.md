## Use Cases

- A fine-chemical manufacturer deployed the EdgePLC BL245 on a polymerization reaction line, pairing distributed I/O process monitoring (temperature, pressure, flow) with an LSTM model forecasting deviations 30–60 minutes ahead. Reported results: product-purity consistency up 42%, material waste down ~18%, energy use down ~12%, and response time shifting from hours to milliseconds. [Bliiot case study](https://bliiot.com/cases-detail/physical-ai-driven-process-control-innovation-an-ai-it-ot-deeply-integrated-application-case-based-on-edgeplc-bl245-industrial-ai-edge-controller)
