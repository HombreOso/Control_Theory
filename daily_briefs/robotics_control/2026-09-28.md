## New Inventions
- Buildroid AI's automated bricklaying system (announced Dec 2025, first US jobsite deployments in 2026) uses NVIDIA Omniverse digital twins with hierarchical task-network planning for high-level sequencing and behavior trees for local task execution, coordinating 40+ supported robot types. It places blocks up to 40 kg and can build walls up to 4 m wide and 3 m tall, and automatically redistributes tasks if a robot drops offline.
