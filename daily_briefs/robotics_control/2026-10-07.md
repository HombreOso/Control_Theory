## Use Cases
- BuiltWorlds' 2026 Annual Equipment & Robotics Benchmarking Report found jobsite robotics usage among contractors jumped to 79% in 2026 (up from 29% in 2025); accuracy was the top cited benefit, with Boston Dynamics most-adopted and Dusty Robotics most piloted. [BD+C](https://www.bdcnetwork.com/aec-tech/article/55395870/adoption-of-jobsite-robotics-doubles-in-2026-builtworlds-report)

## Research Papers
- "Tripody: An Overconstrained 3-SPR-like Parallel Robot for High-Reach Construction Tasks" (ETH Zürich) describes a 33kg wheeled parallel robot extending 1.7–3.4m reach with 32kg payload for ceiling/overhead construction work, achieving sub-0.6mm closed-loop positioning. [arXiv:2607.25781](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25781)
