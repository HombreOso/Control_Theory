# Control Systems Brief — Robotics Control — 2026-09-30

## Tech News
- Construction-robotics funding is surging: Gravis Robotics raised a $200M Series A from SoftBank, Bedrock Robotics raised $270M in Series B (deploying fully autonomous excavators for contractors like Sundt and Zachry Construction), and FieldAI has raised $405M across rounds, with Nvidia (and Nventures) and Bill Gates among the backers — SoftBank framed the push as investment in "physical AI." Experts note data quality ("garbage in, garbage out") remains the main obstacle, with small robots doing repetitive tasks showing the most success so far. [New construction robots gain traction on jobsites](https://www.constructiondive.com/news/construction-robotics-adoption-investment-nvidia-gravis-bedrock-field-ai/828294/) (Aug 19, 2026)
- BuiltWorlds' 2026 Equipment & Robotics Benchmarking Report found 79% of surveyed contractors now use robotics to some degree and pilot-only participation nearly tripled to 32% year-over-year; accuracy (75%), reducing manual labor (63%), and safety (56%) were the top adoption drivers, with FieldAI, Boston Dynamics, and Dusty Robotics called out as standouts. [BuiltWorlds Survey Finds Surge of Robotics Adoption Among Contractors](https://concreteproducts.com/index.php/2026/09/21/builtworlds-survey-finds-surge-of-robotics-adoption-among-contractors/) (Sept 21, 2026)

## Research Papers
- "Humanoid Loco-Manipulation With Discrete VLA Model" develops a discrete vision-language-action model for coordinated whole-body locomotion and manipulation in humanoid robots. [arXiv:2609.35709](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35709)
- "Uni-VLaT: Whole-Body Tactile Adaptation of VLA Policies for Humanoid Loco-Manipulation" integrates tactile feedback into VLA policies to improve combined movement-and-manipulation control for humanoids. [arXiv:2609.35450](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35450)
- "LQR-ArUco Fusion: Robust Hierarchical Control for Navigation and Asymmetric Manipulation in Two-Wheeled Robots" combines an LQR controller with ArUco visual-marker localization in a hierarchical control scheme for mobile-robot navigation plus manipulation. [arXiv:2609.35700](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35700)
- "Graph-Based Simultaneous Path and Foothold Planning for Multi-Limbed Intra-Vehicular Robots in Space Stations" addresses coupled path/foothold motion planning for multi-limbed robots operating in constrained spacecraft interiors — a control problem structurally similar to legged/climbing robots navigating cluttered construction sites. [arXiv:2609.35000](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35000)
