## Research Papers
- "Learning Slope-Adaptive Whole-Body Locomotion for Humanoid Robots in Roofing Construction" (Sept 17, 2026) grounds retargeted human motion capture to a 3D roof model and uses RL so a humanoid maintains correct foot/hand placement while nailgunning, hammering, and walking on pitched roofs — a directly construction-relevant control paper. [arXiv:2609.20558](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20558)
- "Agile-WAM: An Agile Tactile World Action Model for Contact-Rich Robot Control" (Sept 17, 2026) processes vision and touch at different rates to control contact-rich manipulation, reporting a 29.4% relative success-rate gain with 11.9 ms inference latency on real hardware. [arXiv:2609.20761](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20761)
- "DexTouch-WM: Learning Action-Conditioned Tactile World Models from Human Touch for Dexterous Robot Manipulation" (Sept 17, 2026, accepted to IROS 2026) supervises a tactile world model with human touch data retargeted to robot hands to improve dexterous manipulation. [arXiv:2609.20649](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20649)
