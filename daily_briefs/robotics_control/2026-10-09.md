## Research Papers
- "Collision-Aware Humanoid Whole-Body Control under Imperfect Tracking Targets" introduces RECAL, a wrapper layer for humanoid whole-body controllers that uses point-cloud cross-attention to balance motion-tracking against collision avoidance, demonstrated on a real Digit V3 humanoid. [arXiv:2609.16405](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16405)
