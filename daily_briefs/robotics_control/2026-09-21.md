# Control Systems Brief — Robotics Control — 2026-09-21
_Note: the research items below were posted to arXiv on Sept 17 but are the current top-trending robot-manipulation papers as of today (Sept 21) per Hugging Face's daily papers tracker; no later-dated robotics-control items could be confirmed today._

## Research Papers
- "SkipVLA: Skipping VLA Steps with Classical Planning for Fast Robot Manipulation" fuses classical motion planning (for free-space motion) with a VLA model queried only for contact-rich skills like grasping, reporting up to 2.5x faster task completion with lower energy use and equivalent success rates. [arXiv:2609.20648](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20648)
- "DeformSmith: Physics Harness-Guided Hierarchical Generation of Deformable Assets for Robot Manipulation" automatically builds and refines physically plausible deformable simulation assets (geometry, materials, dynamics) for robot manipulation from text or a single image. [arXiv:2609.18620](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18620)
- "Learning Foresight without Explicit Trajectories for 3D Diffusion Policies" introduces Movement Trend Guidance, a lightweight latent-conditioning method that gives 3D diffusion manipulation policies anticipatory "foresight," improving success rates on RoboTwin2.0, LIBERO-40, and real-robot tasks with only 3.52% added parameters. [arXiv:2609.20669](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20669)
