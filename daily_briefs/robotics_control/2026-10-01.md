# Control Systems Brief — Robotics Control — 2026-10-01

## Tech News

- Autonomous Solutions Inc. (ASI) and SoftBank Group formed a joint venture, backed by $225M from SoftBank, to commercialize autonomous heavy equipment for large infrastructure projects. ASI's Mobius platform is a brand-agnostic fleet-orchestration system coordinating haul trucks, dozers, loaders, and compactors from different manufacturers on shared construction tasks. [Tackling construction labor shortages: ASI and SoftBank partner on autonomous fleets](https://therobotreport.com/tackling-construction-labor-shortages-asi-softbank-partner-autonomous-fleets) (Sept 30, 2026)
- A roundup of construction robotics adoption finds contractor pilot programs nearly tripling and robotics usage up 45% year-over-year, led by Bedrock Robotics (autonomous excavators), Gravis Robotics ($200M Series A), and FieldAI ($405M raised for a general-purpose "robot brain"); backers include Nvidia, Bill Gates, and Tishman Speyer. [New construction robots gain traction on jobsites](https://www.constructiondive.com/news/construction-robotics-adoption-investment-nvidia-gravis-bedrock-field-ai/828294/) (Aug 19, 2026)

## New Inventions

- Gravis Robotics (spun out of ETH Zurich) raised a $200M Series A from SoftBank and detailed its product line: Gravis Rack (a retrofit autonomous-control kit for excavators from Caterpillar, John Deere, and others), Gravis Copilot (AI-assisted manual control with real-time 3D guidance), and Slate (a worksite interface for directing autonomous operations). The system reportedly improves productivity up to 30% and works across mixed equipment fleets without custom reprogramming. [Gravis Robotics raises $200M for autonomous construction](https://www.therobotreport.com/gravis-robotics-raises-200m-autonomous-construction/) (Aug 17, 2026)
- Destro AI raised an $8M seed round (Base10 Partners, Bonfire Ventures) for a robot-agnostic warehouse fleet-coordination platform combining MothershipOS (cross-hardware task assignment) and VisionOS (on-robot mobile manipulation using models trained from human demonstrations); already deployed commercially with Yusen Logistics Americas. [Destro AI Raises $8M in Seed Funding to Coordinate Warehouse Robot Fleets](https://theaiinsider.tech/2026/09/30/destro-ai-raises-8m-in-seed-funding-to-coordinate-warehouse-robot-fleets/) (Sept 30, 2026)

## Use Cases

- Bedrock Robotics' "Operator" retrofit package — 360-degree perception plus automatic stop-on-obstacle — is running fully autonomous excavators on a Nevada water treatment project (Sundt Construction) and a 1.2M cubic yard civil sitework job (Zachry Construction), following a year of supervised testing before autonomous authorization. [Bedrock Robotics deploys fully autonomous excavators on jobsites](https://www.constructiondive.com/news/bedrock-robotics-fully-autonomous-excavators-jobsites/828267/) (Aug 19, 2026)
