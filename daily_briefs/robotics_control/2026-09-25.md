# Control Systems Brief — Robotics Control — 2026-09-25

## Tech News
- [BuiltWorlds survey finds surge of robotics adoption among contractors](https://concreteproducts.com/index.php/2026/09/21/builtworlds-survey-finds-surge-of-robotics-adoption-among-contractors/) (Sept 21, 2026) — BuiltWorlds' 2026 survey found 79% of contractors now use jobsite robotics and 32% have piloted automation on at least one site (up from 12% in 2025), driven by accuracy improvement (75%), reduced manual labor (63%), and safety (56%). Named leaders: FieldAI (highest-rated), Boston Dynamics (most adopted), Dusty Robotics (most piloted), Advanced Construction Robotics (automated rebar tying), and Cobod/Icon (concrete 3D printing). See also: [2026 Robotics Top 50 - BuiltWorlds](https://builtworlds.com/insights/2026-robotics-50/).

## Software & Tools
- [Intrinsic open-sources core industrial robotics technology (ROSCon 2026)](https://www.unite.ai/intrinsic-open-sources-core-robotics-capabilities-at-roscon-2026/) (Sept 22, 2026) — Intrinsic released "Intrinsic Core" under Apache 2.0, including ICON (a real-time hardware-agnostic control framework letting robots adapt mid-trajectory to environment changes), automatic collision-free motion planning, sensor-driven grasp planning, 6-DoF pose estimation via NVIDIA FoundationPose, and Gazebo simulation with ROS 2-compatible drivers.
- [Robotiq releases open-source software packages for the physical AI ecosystem](https://www.roboticstomorrow.com/news/2026/09/22/robotiq-releases-open-source-software-packages-for-the-physical-ai-ecosystem/27136) (Sept 22, 2026) — Robotiq released a C++ SDK, a ROS 2 driver package, and NVIDIA Isaac Sim assets (with closed-loop kinematics) for its adaptive grippers, part of its "Contact Core" physical-AI layer, aimed at tighter real-hardware control loops and higher-fidelity manipulation simulation/training.

- [Multi-Skill Robot Learning Through Functional Decomposition and Spatial Sequencing for Construction Finishing](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7083099) (SSRN, Yoon, Park, Ahn) — Proposes decomposing construction-finishing tasks into functional robot skills and sequencing them spatially, a construction-robotics-specific control/learning approach.
