## Software & Tools

- Robotiq released official ROS 2 packages (v1.1.0, 2026-08-26) for its 2F-85/2F-140 adaptive grippers — a drop-in replacement for the community driver, with 200 Hz control frequency, support across Humble/Jazzy/Lyrical distributions from one codebase, and a shared open-source C++ SDK core for ROS and non-ROS integration. [Robotiq blog](https://blog.robotiq.com/robotiq-releases-ros-2-packages-for-adaptive-grippers)

## Use Cases

- TUM researchers (Chair of Computing in Civil and Building Engineering) trained a Proximal Policy Optimization RL controller to grasp cuboid objects at arbitrary positions, as a first step toward automating crane-load stabilization on construction sites in place of manual load-guiding. Initial experiments show robustness to previously unseen object positions, with some spatial regions still needing further work. Presented at ISARC 2026 (Singapore, June 2026). [TUM](https://portal.fis.tum.de/en/publications/towards-rl-control-for-flexible-robotic-applications-in-construct/)

## Research Papers
