# Control Systems Brief — Robotics Control — 2026-10-06

## New Inventions
- **Robot.com commercially launched "R-noid,"** a general-purpose humanoid robot aimed at labor-intensive roles (food service, logistics, hospitality) facing staffing shortages. [Robotics 24/7 coverage](https://www.robotics247.com/article/automate-2026-robot.com-launches-r-noid-humanoid-robot)
- **RIC Robotics is preparing "Zyrex," a 20-foot autonomous construction robot** for welding, carpentry, 3D printing, and material handling, offered via leasing from under $20,000/month. [dig.watch coverage](https://dig.watch/updates/autonomous-construction-robot-zyrex-set-for-2026-debut)

## Use Cases
- A construction-market analysis reports the **first humanoid robot deployment on an active construction site**, handling material transport and repetitive tasks on uneven terrain — described by analysts as a "structural inflection point" from experimental pilots to early commercialization. [Next Move Strategy Consulting](https://www.nextmsc.com/news/humanoid-robot-deployment-reshapes-construction-market)

## Research Papers
- **"Hierarchical Reinforcement Learning for Collision-Free Locomotion of an Underactuated Biped"** (Sahoo, Kumar, S.K., Datta, Dwivedi, Shukla; submitted 6 Oct 2026) presents a hierarchical RL approach for safe, collision-free bipedal locomotion. [arXiv:2610.05855](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05855)
- **"Transporting Unsecured Stacked Payloads with a Quadrupedal Robot via Multi-Objective Reinforcement Learning"** (Namura, Hiromoto, Uemura, Sasaki, Suzuki; submitted 6 Oct 2026) uses multi-objective RL to let a quadruped carry unsecured stacked loads without them toppling — directly relevant to jobsite material handling. [arXiv:2610.05819](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05819)
- **"DexForge: High-Fidelity Physics-Informed Dexterous Retargeting"** (Wang, Cao, Ni, Xie, Hong; submitted 6 Oct 2026) introduces a physics-informed method for retargeting human hand motion to dexterous robot manipulators. [arXiv:2610.06331](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06331)
- **"AffordCraft: Scalable Construction of Task-Ready Simulation Assets from Single Images"** (Yang, Zhou, Xie, Chen; submitted 6 Oct 2026) generates task-ready, affordance-aware simulation assets from single images to scale up robot training environments — useful for construction-robotics sim-to-real pipelines. [arXiv:2610.06643](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06643)
