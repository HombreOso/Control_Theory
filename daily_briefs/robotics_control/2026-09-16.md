## Research Papers
- "JEPLO: Joint-Embedding Predictive Learning for LiDAR-Based Legged Locomotion" (Sept 14, 2026) applies joint-embedding predictive learning to LiDAR-conditioned legged locomotion control. [arXiv:2609.15770](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15770)
- "Weave: Learning Whole-Body Dexterous Loco-Manipulation from Human-Object Interactions" (Sept 15, 2026) learns whole-body dexterous loco-manipulation skills directly from human-object interaction data. [arXiv:2609.16683](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16683)
- "Learning Holistic Whole-Body Loco-Manipulation with a Bipedal Mobile Manipulator" (Sept 16, 2026) trains a unified RL controller that maps 6-DoF end-effector targets to coordinated bipedal-base-plus-arm actions, validated on hardware with VR teleoperation and learned-policy command sources. [arXiv:2609.18930](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18930)
- "Primitive-Informed Sampling-Based MPC for Multi-Fingered Dexterous Manipulation" (submitted Sept 14, revised Sept 16, 2026) biases sampling-based MPC with low-dimensional finger-motion primitives for in-hand rotation on a 16-DoF Allegro hand. [arXiv:2609.14868](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14868)
