## Use Cases
- Dutch construction-robotics startup Monumental completed a project in Kesteren, Netherlands where its autonomous bricklaying robots laid over 80% of the bricks — its first project done entirely on progressive scaffolding, raising robots 2 metres to keep working at height. [IAmsterdam Startup Map](https://startupmap.iamsterdam.com/news/note/monumental-s-robots-lay-80-of-bricks-on-kesteren-project)

## Research Papers
- "Vision Guided Target Conditioned Control for Autonomous Excavation" presents a transformer-based policy that drives an excavator bucket toward a vision-specified target using multi-camera images and joint sensors; in simulated pile-clearing the method cleared 76.8% of a pile versus 27.4%/15.7% for baselines, reaching 91% of human-level efficiency. [arXiv:2608.21778](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21778)
