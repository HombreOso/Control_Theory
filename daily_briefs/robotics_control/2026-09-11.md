# Control Systems Brief — Robotics Control — 2026-09-11

## Tech News
- Bedrock Robotics moved its autonomous excavator system, "Bedrock Operator," from testing into commercial deployment on live U.S. construction sites (Sept 7, 2026). The retrofit system (sensors + onboard compute + ML) lets excavators perceive their environment, plan movements, and execute tasks without an onboard operator, with automatic stops when unauthorized people/objects enter safety zones. [CCE Online News](https://cceonlinenews.com/technology/bedrock-puts-autonomous-excavators-to-work-on-live-construction-sites/)

## New Inventions
- Built Robotics' Exosystem retrofits mid-size excavators from major manufacturers with an all-weather sensor/compute enclosure (radar, 360° cameras, RTK GPS, IMUs) for autonomous or manual operation; installation takes a few hours and is fully reversible, available via rental starting at three months. [IronPros](https://www.ironpros.com/product-categories/technology-and-software/equipment-automation/construction-robotics/product/22900327/built-robotics-exosystem)

## Use Cases
- Bedrock's autonomous excavators are active on a Nevada water treatment facility, a Texas earthwork project, and a 1.2-million-cubic-yard civil works project with Zachry Construction and Sundt Construction; the company's stated roadmap is coordinating multiple autonomous machine types as one fleet. [CCE Online News](https://cceonlinenews.com/technology/bedrock-puts-autonomous-excavators-to-work-on-live-construction-sites/)

## Research Papers
- "Actuator Reality Shaping for Zero-Shot Sim-to-Real Robot Learning" (July 2026) shapes physical actuators' closed-loop response to match idealized simulation dynamics via a two-DOF controller per joint, enabling RL policies trained in simulation to transfer to real hardware (servo, 7-DOF arm, wheeled-legged robot, humanoid) without fine-tuning. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02205)
- "Simulator Adaptation for Sim-to-Real Learning of Legged Locomotion via Proprioceptive Distribution Matching" (April 2026) adapts the simulator itself using proprioceptive data to close the sim-to-real gap for legged locomotion controllers. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.11090)
