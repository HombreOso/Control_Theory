# Control Systems Brief — Robotics Control — 2026-09-15
_Backfilled brief — written retrospectively on 2026-09-21 for this date._

## Tech News
- Researchers at UC Berkeley and Stanford (with Unitree) published BeyondMimic, a framework that teaches a Unitree G1 humanoid to sprint, cartwheel, and perform martial-arts kicks from ~2.5 hours of human motion-capture data, combining motion-tracking policies with guided diffusion; the underlying Science Robotics paper appeared Aug 26 and the coverage broke Sept 10, 2026. [Humanoid robot learns to sprint and perform spin kicks using AI trained on human motion data](https://techxplore.com/news/2026-09-humanoid-robot-sprint-ai-human.html)

## New Inventions
- Universal Robots unveiled its seventh-generation platform (three new arms plus a redesigned controller/teach pendant) at IMTS, adding impedance control, built-in tool-flange force-torque sensing, and RTDE for force-sensitive applications, with 40% more controller compute in a 30% smaller footprint. [Universal Robots launches its seventh generation robot platform at IMTS](https://www.therobotreport.com/universal-robots-launches-its-seventh-generation-robot-platform-at-imts/)

## Use Cases
- Bedrock Robotics (founded by ex-Waymo engineers) began fully autonomous excavator deployments on live US construction sites with Sundt Construction, Champion Site Prep, and Zachry Construction, using a retrofit perception/planning stack with safety stop behavior; announced Sept 1, 2026 and covered widely through mid-September. [Bedrock Robotics launches first fully autonomous excavator deployments on critical US infrastructure](https://www.intelligentbuild.tech/2026/09/01/bedrock-robotics-launches-first-fully-autonomous-excavator-deployments-on-critical-us/)

[arXiv:2609.08958](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08958)
