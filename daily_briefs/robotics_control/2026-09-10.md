## Use Cases
- **2026 Construction Robotics Report** (Zacua Ventures, Hilti Ventures, 94 Ventures). Argues construction robotics has moved from pilot to production in four workflows: robotic layout/measurement printing (5–8x faster than manual, ±3mm precision), autonomous solar-pile driving (25–40% faster, <0.5% rework), rebar-tying robots (300–450 ties/hr vs. 40–80 manual), and drone/scanner digital capture — with "robotics-as-a-service" (per-task/per-m²) pricing emerging as the dominant business model over capital purchase. [Zacua Ventures](https://zacuaventures.com/construction-robotics-report-2026/) ([secondary coverage](https://prefabnews.com.au/construction-robotics-2026-report-australia/))
- **"Construction Robotics Works Where Variation Ends."** Contends on-site robotics still represents under 0.03% of global construction spend and only succeeds in low-variation, repeatable tasks. Highlights Hyperion Robotics (3D-printing structural components off-site — substation foundations for National Grid exceeding strength targets, concrete rail sleepers for Costain's East Coast Cluster; raised $7.4M in July 2026) as the strongest current success case, contrasted with the abandoned 1980s–90s Japanese automated-construction systems (Shimizu SMART, Obayashi ABCS). [AEC Business, Aug 3, 2026](https://aec-business.com/construction-robotics-works-where-variation-ends/)

## Research Papers
- **"TANGO: Humanoid Navigation in Cluttered Environments with a Whole-Body Vision-Language-Action Model"** — a whole-body VLA model coordinating arms/torso/gait for humanoid navigation from language + vision, outputting 29-DoF joint commands; trained purely in simulation and validated zero-shot on a real Unitree G1. [arXiv:2609.09158](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09158)
- **"Online, Reachability-Aware, Sampling-Based Motion Planning"** — an online interval-based method for computing reachable-set approximations for sampling-based MPC without expensive pre-computation; reports >99% reduction in safety violations in racing simulation and successful real-hardware racecar control. [arXiv:2609.09073](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09073)
- **"DeCAL: Towards Physically-Grounded Dexterous Vision-Language-Action Models via Contact-Aware Latent Co-Imagination"** — a Mixture-of-Transformers VLA model for contact-rich dexterous manipulation with tactile-fusion and co-imagination of visual/tactile dynamics; reports 71% average task success rate. [arXiv:2609.09119](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09119)
- **"Model Predictive Control of Tensegrity Robots"** — MPC for soft tensegrity robots using a contact-aware graph neural network dynamics model. [arXiv:2609.08958](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08958)
